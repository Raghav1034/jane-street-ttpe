# TTPE Competition Requirements Checklist

Tracks every requirement from the Jane Street Protocol Emulator ASIC Competition against the current repository state.

**Primary sources (latest organizer text takes precedence over earlier drafts):**
* **Blog & Rules (updated 2026-11-09):** *Can you design a chip? Announcing the protocol emulator ASIC competition* (`https://blog.janestreet.com/protocol-emulator-asic-competition/`, 9 Nov organizer email confirming 8×4 tiles and `TinyTapeout/ttihp-verilog-template` `main` targeting CMOS5L).
* **Competition FAQ:** `#faq` on the competition blog post (sections *Entering*, *Design*, *Submission and judging*).
* **SRAM Reference:** Ken Pettit, *Dropping a real SRAM macro into your CMOS5L tile* (2026-09-29).
* **Deadline:** **January 18, 2027** (March 2027 CMOS5L shuttle).

**Status legend:** `DONE` = requirement met with verifiable evidence; `PARTIAL` = in progress or open items remain; `MISSING` = not yet created; `OUT OF SCOPE` = explicitly excluded with rationale.
